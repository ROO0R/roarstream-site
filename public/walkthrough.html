[GUIDE] Link's Awakening — Walkthrough Command Center (GBC)

[CH1] Chapter 1 — Tail Cave

1.1 — The Boy on the Shore

  • Name your save file. (Tip: Enter "ZELDA" for bonus music.)
  • Talk to Tarin inside Marin's House → Receive [Shield].
  • Exit house. Go LEFT 2 screens, then DOWN 5 screens → Toronbo Shores.
  • Use [Shield] to push the Sea Urchin out of the path.
  • Pick up [Sword].
  • Go LEFT 2 screens. Defeat Octoroks with sword.
  • Head NORTH back toward Mabe Village.
  • WARNING: Enter the well on the screen with the two boys — DROP from the UPPER LEDGE. Pick up [Heart Piece #1] (Permanent Missable if you skip).
  • Go UP 2 screens → Enter Mysterious Forest.
▶ Current Scene: At the entrance to the Mysterious Forest with Shield, Sword, and Heart Piece #1 collected.

1.2 — Mysterious Forest

  • Go UP 1. Defeat Moblins (2 sword slashes each).
  • Go RIGHT 1 → UP 1 → RIGHT 1. Defeat Moblin. Enter cave on the right.
  • Inside cave: Defeat Keese. Slash blue crystals. Push stone block away from chest. Open chest → 50 Rupees.
  • Go UP 1. Kill Green Zols. Go LEFT 1. (Heart Piece visible — ignore for now.)
  • Exit cave BELOW. Outside: Pick up [Toadstool].
  • Re-enter cave. Push ALL blocks RIGHT, then push top-right block UP once. Exit cave.
  • Go UP 1 → RIGHT 1 → Fairy Fountain (heal if needed).
  • Go LEFT 1 → DOWN 1 → RIGHT 2 → Exit forest.
  • Go DOWN 1. Ignore River Zora & Buzz Blob. Go DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1 → Enter Witch's Hut.
  • Assign [Toadstool] to a button. Give it to the Witch → Receive [Magic Powder].
  • Exit hut. Go LEFT 1 → UP 1 → UP 1 → LEFT 1 → Re-enter Mysterious Forest.
  • Forest path to Raccoon: LEFT 1 → DOWN 2 → LEFT 1 → UP 1
  • Raccoon Puzzle: Sprinkle [Magic Powder] on the Raccoon → reveals Tarin.
  • Go UP 1 → Open chest → [Tail Key].
  • Exit forest: DOWN 2 → LEFT 1 → DOWN 2
▶ Current Scene: Outside Mysterious Forest with the Tail Key in inventory.

1.3 — Side-quests in Mabe Village

  • Find Fisherman: 1 WEST + 2 NORTH of Marin's House. Pay 10 Rupees to fish.
  • WARNING: Catch the large fish directly beneath Link → [Heart Piece #2] (Missable — get it now).
  • Go to screen RIGHT of Marin's House. Cut bushes → [Secret Seashell #1].
  • Enter Trendy Game Shop (bottom-right of village). Pay 10 Rupees.
  • Crane Game: Hold B until crane reaches center, then hold A until shadow is over the Yoshi Doll → Receive [Yoshi Doll].
  • From Trendy Game: UP 3 → LEFT 1. Enter large house. Give [Yoshi Doll] to mother → Receive [Ribbon].
  • Go to small house WEST of Marin's House. Give [Ribbon] to Small Chain Chomp → Receive [Dog Food].
  • Head to Toronbo Shores (south-west). Go RIGHT 3. Enter house. Give [Dog Food] to Crocodile → Receive [Bananas].
▶ Current Scene: Have Heart Piece #2, Seashell #1, Bananas, and the Tail Key. Ready for Tail Cave.

1.4 — Tail Cave

  • Path to entrance from Toronbo Shore: RIGHT 1 → UP 2 → RIGHT 2 → DOWN 1 → LEFT 1 → DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1
  • Use [Tail Key] on center statue → Enter dungeon.
  • Go LEFT 1. Knock both Hardhat Beetles off ledge. Pick up [Small Key].
  • Go LEFT 1. Defeat 4 Green Zols. Open chest → [Compass].
  • Go RIGHT 2 → UP 1. Step on floor switch → chest → [Small Key].
  • Go RIGHT 1. Corner and slash Stalfos x2 (2 hits each). Defeat Keese → chest → [Dungeon Map].
  • Go UP 1 through one-way door. Defeat enemies. Use [Small Key] on locked door → go RIGHT.

[OPT] Optional — Stone Beak

  • Avoid Sparks → go UP 1.
  • Three-of-a-Kind Puzzle: Slash all three enemies until they all show the same suit (heart/diamond/spade/club). Watch the cycling pattern → Chest → [Stone Beak].
  • Go DOWN 2 → LEFT 2.

[ITEM] Getting Roc's Feather

  • Defeat Mini-Moldorm → Open chest → [Small Key].
  • Go LEFT 1. Defeat Mini-Moldorm. (Cracked wall here — needs Bombs, return later.)
  • Go UP 1. Push block UP 1 to avoid Blade Trap. Use [Small Key] on locked door.
  • Go RIGHT 1. Maneuver through blocks, defeat Gels. Push lone block RIGHT → door opens.
  • Spiked Beetles: Hold out [Shield] → let beetles run into it to flip them → slash with sword. Defeat both → staircase. Go DOWN.
  • Side-scroll area: Slash/jump-stomp Goombas. Go LEFT 1, defeat Goomba, climb stairs.
  • Go UP 2. Avoid Blade Traps. Open chest → [Roc's Feather]! Link can now jump.
  • Go DOWN 1. Grab floating heart. Go DOWN 1 → backtrack through Goomba side-scroll.
▶ Current Scene: Roc's Feather acquired. Backtracking to find Nightmare Key.

[KEY] Getting the Nightmare Key

  • Go RIGHT 1. Use [Roc's Feather] to jump over the pit (shortcut).
  • Go DOWN 2 → RIGHT 1 → RIGHT 1 (upper pathway).
  • Go UP 1. Jump the gap with [Roc's Feather]. Use [Small Key] on locked block.
  • Go LEFT 1. Open chest → [Nightmare Key].
  • Backtrack: RIGHT 1 → DOWN 1 → LEFT 1 → RIGHT 1 (lower path) → RIGHT 1
  • Jump over gap on RIGHT with [Roc's Feather] → Enter Mini-Boss room.

[BOSS] Mini-Boss: Rolling Bones

  • Solution: Jump over the rolling spiked log with [Roc's Feather]. Slash him as he crosses. He always jumps the same diagonal — predict his position. Hit him 8 times → defeated.
  • Collect Fairy (heal up). Step into warp portal if needed. Go UP 1. Skip staircase. Avoid Blade Traps.
  • Use [Nightmare Key] on locked door → Enter Boss room.

[BOSS] Boss: Moldorm

  • Solution: Target the glowing tail. Use [Roc's Feather] to jump OVER Moldorm when he charges. Slash the tail mid-jump or on approach. Hit tail 4 times → defeated.
  • WARNING: If you fall off the ledge, you restart the fight. Stay away from edges.
  • Pick up [Heart Container]. Go UP 1 → Collect [Full Moon Cello] — Instrument #1! ♫
▶ Current Scene: Tail Cave complete. Full Moon Cello, Heart Container, Roc's Feather, 2 Heart Pieces. Head to Chapter 2 — Bottle Grotto.

[CH2] Chapter 2 — Bottle Grotto

2.1 — Dognapped

  • Exit Tail Cave. Go LEFT 1 → meet the Owl. Go UP 1. Jump holes with [Roc's Feather].
  • Go LEFT 2 → UP 1 → back to Mabe Village.
  • Enter Mysterious Forest: LEFT 1 → UP 2 from village entrance.
  • Forest path to exit: UP 2 → RIGHT 2 → UP 2 → RIGHT 2 → Exit forest.
  • WARNING: Jump gap with [Roc's Feather] → Pick up [Heart Piece #3] (Missable now that you have Roc's Feather).
  • Jump back over gap. Go UP 1 → RIGHT 1 → Enter Moblin's Hideout cave.
  • Room 1: Sidestep sword Moblin to LEFT or RIGHT flank. Slash to defeat. Go UP 1.
  • Room 2: Defeat 4 arrow Moblins. Go RIGHT 1.
  • King Moblin: Dodge 2–4 arrows (use [Shield] or sidestep). When he charges, sidestep → he rams the wall and stuns. Slash him. Repeat 8 times.
  • Collect Fairy. Go RIGHT 1 → Walk to BowWow to rescue him. Exit hideout.
▶ Current Scene: BowWow rescued and following Link. Heart Piece #3 collected.

2.2 — Goponga Swamp

[OPT] Optional — Heart Piece #4 (Cemetery)

  • From Moblin Hideout: RIGHT 1 → DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1 → DOWN 2 → Enter Cemetery.
  • Go DOWN 1 → LEFT 1 → reach bottom-left of graveyard.
  • Gravestone Puzzle: Push the bottom-right gravestone UP → staircase appears. Go DOWN.
  • Inside: Push bottom block RIGHT. Use [Roc's Feather] for a diagonal jump to the right platform. Jump UP → Pick up [Heart Piece #4].
  • Jump back, climb stairs. Backtrack: UP 1 → LEFT 1 → LEFT 1 → UP 2 → LEFT 1 → Re-enter Mysterious Forest.
  • Forest shortcut: LEFT 3 → UP 1 → Exit Mysterious Forest.
  • Go RIGHT 1 (phone booth here). Go RIGHT 1 → Enter Goponga Swamp.
  • Go UP 1 → RIGHT 1. Let BowWow eat Goponga Flowers.
  • Go DOWN 1 (right side). BowWow eats Giant Goponga Flower. Go RIGHT 1 → Open chest → 50 Rupees.
  • Backtrack LEFT 1 → UP 1 → RIGHT 1. BowWow eats the 5 Goponga Flowers → Enter Bottle Grotto. (BowWow stays outside.)
▶ Current Scene: At entrance of Bottle Grotto. 4 Heart Pieces, 5 full hearts. BowWow waiting outside.

2.3 — Bottle Grotto

  • Room 1: Ignore chest surrounded by pots for now. Go UP 1.
  • Torch Puzzle: Use [Magic Powder] to light both torches → right door opens. Avoid Spark. Go RIGHT 1.
  • Defeat 2 Stalfos (sidestep their air-stomp, then slash). Collect [Small Key]. Go LEFT 1.
  • Use [Small Key] on left locked door. Avoid Blade Traps. Defeat 2 Keese. Go UP 1.
  • Knock Hardhat Beetle into abyss. (Tip: Lure it left, run right, jump the gap.) → Door at bottom unlocks.
  • Open chest → [Stone Beak]. Backtrack DOWN 1 → RIGHT 2.
  • Hit Crystal Switch once. Go DOWN 1.
  • Defeat Shrouded Stalfos. Hit Crystal Switch. Open chest → [Small Key]. Go RIGHT 1.
  • Jump gaps with [Roc's Feather]. Grab floating Magic Powder. Jump to bottom-right → Step on switch → chest → [Small Key]. Go UP 1.
  • Mask-Mimic Puzzle: Face AWAY from them — they mimic your moves. Perform a spin attack up close to defeat both. Key drops on LEFT side — can't reach yet.
  • Backtrack: DOWN 1 → LEFT 1 → UP 1 → RIGHT 1 → collect the dropped [Small Key].
  • Backtrack LEFT 2 → Use [Small Key] on locked door at bottom. Defeat lone Mask-Mimic. Open chest → [Compass].
  • Backtrack: UP 1 → RIGHT 1 → DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1 → UP 1
▶ Current Scene: Have Stone Beak, Compass, and 2 Small Keys. Heading toward the Mini-Boss.

Approaching Hinox

  • Go RIGHT 1. Hold out [Shield] → beetles flip → slash to defeat. Go UP through locked door.
  • Defeat Keese. Avoid Spark.
  • Block Puzzle: Push the 2 blocks toward the center to cover both blue floor blocks → staircase at top-right. Go DOWN.
  • Navigate side-scroll: Use [Roc's Feather] to clear spikes. Climb ladder → resurface.
  • Defeat Keese. Jump to center platform. Grab floating heart. Go UP through one-way door → Mini-Boss: Hinox.
  • Hinox: Keep distance. Move in for single sword slashes. When he charges, sidestep. Avoid cracked tiles. Defeat him → Fairy appears.
  • Collect Fairy. Go RIGHT 1. Defeat 2 Keese. Do NOT go down staircase. Go UP 1.
  • Vacuum Enemy: Dash in with [Roc's Feather] and slash it. (Do NOT land on top — teleports you to dungeon start.)
  • Open chest → [Dungeon Map]. Go UP 1. Open chest → 20 Rupees. Grab floating Magic Powder. Go LEFT through locked door.
  • Boo Buddies: Do NOT slash them — use [Magic Powder] to light both torches → Boos float away. Open chest → [Power Bracelet]! (Can now lift pots, stones, skulls.)
  • Step into warp portal → return to dungeon entrance. Lift pots blocking Room 1 chest → 50 Rupees. Re-enter warp portal.
  • Go RIGHT 1 → UP 2. Lift pots. Walk on blocks. Hit Crystal Switch → walk right → hit Crystal Switch again → chest → [Small Key]. Hit Crystal Switch once more. Walk right on blocks → go RIGHT 1.
  • Defeat Keese/Gel. Knock Hardhat Beetle into abyss. Go RIGHT 1 → through open door at bottom.

[KEY] Getting the Nightmare Key

  • Room Enemy Order (STRICT ORDER):
    1. Move blocks → reach Pols Voice → lift a pot → throw it at the Pols Voice.
    2. Slash the Keese.
    3. Slash the Shrouded Stalfos last.
  • Open chest → [Nightmare Key].
  • Go UP 1 → RIGHT through locked door.
  • Defeat 2 Pols Voice + Zol → staircase at top-right. Go DOWN.
  • Step on block to lower down. Next room: Lift pot, climb stairs, step on block again. Climb steps → resurface.
  • Use [Roc's Feather] to jump gaps. Use [Nightmare Key] on boss door → Enter Boss: Genie.

[BOSS] Boss: Genie

  • Phase 1: Genie is invincible — dodge fireballs by moving left/right. Wait for him to retreat into his bottle.
  • Bottle Phase: Slash bottle to stun → pick it up with [Power Bracelet] → throw it against the wall. Repeat 3 times → bottle shatters.
  • Phase 2: Dodge fireballs (1 full heart each — use [Roc's Feather] to evade faster). Slash Genie when open. Hit him 8 times → defeated.
  • Pick up [Heart Container]. Go LEFT 1 → Collect [Conch Horn] — Instrument #2! ♫
▶ Current Scene: Bottle Grotto complete. Conch Horn, Power Bracelet, Heart Container, 4 Heart Pieces. 6 full hearts. Head to Chapter 3 — Key Cavern.

  • Go DOWN 1 → Enter cave. Go UP 1 → LEFT 1 → Lift skull with [Power Bracelet] → [Heart Piece #5]. Exit cave.

  • Go LEFT 1 → DOWN 2 → Return to Mabe Village. Return BowWow to Madam MeowMeow's house (LEFT 1 from Marin's House).

3.2 — Kanalet Castle

  • From Mabe Village Shop: RIGHT 2 → Find Warp Portal #1. Climb steps and fall in to activate it.
  • Go DOWN 2 → DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1 → DOWN 1 → Enter Richard's Villa. Speak to Richard. (Confirm you have [Bananas].)
  • Path to Kanalet Castle: RIGHT 1 → UP 3 → RIGHT 1 → UP 2 → RIGHT 1 → UP 1
  • Go RIGHT 2 → Find Kiki the Monkey. Give him [Bananas] → Monkeys build bridge.
  • Pick up [Stick] (Trading Sequence Item #5) from bridge. Go UP 1 → UP 2 → LEFT 1. Slash lone bush → Hidden staircase. Go DOWN.
  • Use [Roc's Feather] to stomp Goombas in side-scroll. Climb stairs → Enter castle grounds.
  • Go LEFT 1 → Defeat Darknut. Go DOWN 1.
  • Crow / Golden Leaf #1: Pick up a stone → Throw it at the crow → Crow flies at you → Charge spin attack to defeat (or throw second stone).
  • Go DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1 → Castle entrance. Go RIGHT 1 → UP 1 → Find Mad Bomber.
  • Mad Bomber / Golden Leaf #2: Watch which hole he pops from → Jump over and slash before he throws. Repeat 4 hits.
  • Backtrack DOWN 1 → LEFT 1 → Enter Kanalet Castle. Go LEFT 1. Defeat 2 Darknuts + Zol → Golden Leaf #3 falls from ceiling.
  • Go UP 1 → RIGHT 1. Step on floor switch → Front gate unlocks. Go LEFT 1 → UP stairs → 2nd Floor.
  • Golden Leaf #4: Place a bomb on each mad bomber symbol on the wall → Darknuts emerge from both holes. Defeat the LEFT darknut.
  • Go RIGHT 1 → DOWN 1 → LEFT 1 → Exit castle. Enter the other door.
  • Locked Door Puzzle: Lift a pot → Throw it at the closed door to open it. Go inside.
  • Ball and Chain Trooper / Golden Leaf #5: Dodge his spinning throw → Rush in and slash immediately after he misses. Hit him 8 times.
  • Backtrack through castle and exit through the front gate. All 5 Golden Leaves collected!
▶ Current Scene: All 5 Golden Leaves collected. Back outside Kanalet Castle front gate.

  • Go DOWN 2 → RIGHT 1 → Open chest → [Dungeon Map]. Backtrack to underground room with 4 locked doors.
  • Go LEFT through locked door → Defeat Pairodds + Zols → [Small Key]. Go RIGHT 1.
  • Go DOWN through locked door → Defeat all enemies → [Small Key]. (Lift center pots → grab Bombs — needed soon.) Go UP 1.

[KEY] Getting the Nightmare Key

  • From Dodongo room: DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1 → UP 1 → RIGHT 1. Defeat enemies.
  • Arrow Floor Puzzle: Place a [Bomb] on the wall where the giant floor arrow points → Creates hole below. Drop down.

Getting the Compass

  • Green Bombites: Let timer count to 1 second → slash again to knock them away before they explode. Defeat all → go LEFT through opened door.
  • Defeat enemies → chest → [Compass].
  • Hidden Bomb Wall: Charge sword → Poke left wall until you hear a strange sound → Place [Bomb] → Go LEFT 1.
  • Defeat Red Bombites → [Small Key] drops. Grab it.
  • Backtrack: RIGHT 2 → DOWN 1 → LEFT 1 → DOWN 1 → LEFT 1
  • Use 4 Small Keys on the locked blocks → Go DOWN to staircase area.

  • Pairodds: Dash right at them with [Pegasus Boots] — they can't teleport away in time. Grab recovery heart. Go LEFT 1.
  • Defeat 5 Keese on moving platforms → Extra [Small Key] drops (ignore it). Go UP 1.
  • Use [Nightmare Key] → Enter boss room → Boss: Slime Eyes.

[BOSS] Boss: Slime Eyes

  • Phase 1 — Drop Him: Equip [Pegasus Boots] → Dash into a wall → Slime Eyes falls from ceiling.
  • Phase 2 — Split Him: Slash the central eye repeatedly. When nearly separated, dash through the middle with Pegasus Boots → blob splits into 2 smaller eyes.
  • Phase 3 — Finish Him: Equip [Roc's Feather]. Slash an eye → it jumps to ceiling → Jump with Roc's Feather just BEFORE it slams the ground (ground slam stuns Link). Hit each eye 4 times.
  • Pick up [Heart Container]. Go UP 1 → Collect [Sea Lily's Bell] — Instrument #3! ♫
