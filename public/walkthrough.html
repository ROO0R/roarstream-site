🎮 Link's Awakening – Tail Cave Command Center

📍 1.1 — The Boy on the Shore
  • Name your save file. (Tip: Enter "ZELDA" for bonus music.)
  • Talk to Tarin inside Marin's House → Receive [Shield].
  • Exit house. Go LEFT 2 screens, then DOWN 5 screens → Toronbo Shores.
  • Use [Shield] to push the Sea Urchin out of the path.
  • Pick up [Sword].
  • Go LEFT 2 screens. Defeat Octoroks with sword.
  • Head NORTH back toward Mabe Village.
  • WARNING: Enter the well on the screen with the two boys — DROP from the UPPER LEDGE. Pick up [Heart Piece #1] (Permanent Missable if you skip).
  • Go UP 2 screens → Enter Mysterious Forest.
Current Scene: You should be at the entrance to the Mysterious Forest with your Shield, Sword, and Heart Piece #1 collected.
📍 1.2 — Mysterious Forest
  • Go UP 1 screen. Defeat Moblins (2 sword slashes each).
  • Go RIGHT 1 → UP 1 → RIGHT 1.
  • Defeat Moblin. Enter the cave on the right.
  • Inside cave: Defeat Keese. Slash blue crystals. Push stone block away from chest.
  • Open chest → 50 Rupees.
  • Go UP 1. Kill Green Zols.
  • Go LEFT 1 (Heart Piece visible — ignore for now).
  • Exit cave BELOW.
  • Pick up [Toadstool].
  • Re-enter cave. Push ALL blocks RIGHT, then push top-right block UP once.
  • Exit → UP 1 → RIGHT 1 → Fairy Fountain (heal if needed).
  • LEFT 1 → DOWN 1 → RIGHT 2 → Exit forest.
  • DOWN 1 → ignore enemies.
  • DOWN 1 → RIGHT 1 → Enter Witch's Hut.
  • Give [Toadstool] → Receive [Magic Powder].
  • Exit → LEFT 1 → UP 1.
  • UP 1 → LEFT 1 → Re-enter forest.
  • Path: LEFT 1 → DOWN 2 → LEFT 1 → UP 1.
  • Use [Magic Powder] on Raccoon → reveals Tarin.
  • UP 1 → Open chest → [Tail Key].
  • Exit: DOWN 2 → LEFT 1 → DOWN 2.
Current Scene: Outside forest with Tail Key.
📍 1.3 — Side-quests in Mabe Village
  • Find Fisherman → Pay 10 Rupees.
  • WARNING: Catch large fish → [Heart Piece #2] (Missable).
  • Cut bushes → [Secret Seashell #1].
  • Enter Trendy Game → Pay 10 Rupees.
  • Win [Yoshi Doll].
  • Trade → Receive [Ribbon].
  • Trade → Receive [Dog Food].
  • Trade → Receive [Bananas].
Current Scene: Ready for Tail Cave.
📍 1.4 — Tail Cave
  • Path to entrance → Enter Tail Cave.
  • Use [Tail Key].

🗝️ Dungeon Navigation

  • Get [Small Key].
  • Get [Compass].
  • Activate switch → [Small Key].
  • Get [Dungeon Map].

🪶 Roc's Feather

  • Defeat Mini-Moldorm → [Small Key].
  • Solve block puzzle.
  • Beat Spiked Beetles.
  • Side-scroll section.
  • Open chest → [Roc's Feather].

🗝️ Nightmare Key

  • Jump shortcuts.
  • Unlock block.
  • Get [Nightmare Key].

👾 Mini-Boss: Rolling Bones

  • Jump log, hit 8 times.

💀 Boss: Moldorm

  • Hit tail 4 times.
  • Avoid falling.
  • Collect [Heart Container].
  • Collect [Full Moon Cello].
Current Scene: Tail Cave complete. Proceed to Bottle Grotto.
Link's Awakening Guide - Chapters 2 & 3

🎮 Link's Awakening – Bottle Grotto Command Center

📍 2.1 — Dognapped
  • Exit Tail Cave. Go LEFT 1 screen → meet the Owl.
  • Go UP 1 screen. Use [Roc's Feather] to jump over holes.
  • Go LEFT 2 → UP 1 → back to Mabe Village.
  • Enter Mysterious Forest: LEFT 1 → UP 2.
  • Path: UP 2 → RIGHT 2 → UP 2 → RIGHT 2 → Exit forest.
  • WARNING: Jump gap → [Heart Piece #3] (Missable).
  • Jump back. UP 1 → RIGHT 1 → Moblin Hideout.

👾 Moblin's Hideout

  • Room 1: Sidestep sword Moblin → defeat → UP.
  • Room 2: Defeat 4 Moblins → RIGHT.
  • Boss: King Moblin → dodge arrows, bait charge, hit 8x.
  • Rescue BowWow.
Current Scene: BowWow rescued. Heart Piece #3 obtained.
📍 2.2 — Goponga Swamp

💀 Optional — Heart Piece #4

  • Go to Cemetery.
  • Push bottom-right gravestone → enter.
  • Puzzle jump → collect [Heart Piece #4].

🌿 Swamp Path

  • Forest shortcut → exit.
  • Enter Goponga Swamp.
  • Let BowWow eat flowers.
  • Open chest → 50 Rupees.
  • Clear 5 flowers → enter Bottle Grotto.
Current Scene: Entrance to Bottle Grotto. BowWow waiting outside.
📍 2.3 — Bottle Grotto

🗝️ Opening

  • Light torches with Magic Powder.
  • Defeat enemies → collect Small Key.
  • Get Stone Beak.

🔵 Crystal Switch

  • Toggle switches → collect Small Keys.
  • Mask-Mimic spin attack trick.
  • Get Compass.

⚔️ Hinox

  • Dodge charges → defeat.
  • Avoid staircase → continue.
  • Get Dungeon Map + Rupees.

💡 Power Bracelet

  • Light torches → remove Boos.
  • Get [Power Bracelet].
  • Warp → collect 50 Rupees chest.

🗝️ Nightmare Key

  • Defeat enemies in order:
    1. Pols Voice (throw pot)
    2. Keese
    3. Stalfos
  • Get [Nightmare Key].

💀 Boss: Genie

  • Phase 1: Dodge.
  • Bottle: Throw 3x.
  • Phase 2: Hit 8x.
  • Collect Heart Container + Conch Horn.
Current Scene: Bottle Grotto complete.

🎮 Link's Awakening – Key Cavern Command Center

  • Mad Batter → Powder capacity = 50.

💚 Heart Piece #5

🐚 Seashells #2–#5

  • Collect Seashells #2–#5 via digging, caves, Tail Cave bomb wall.
  • Buy Shovel + Bombs.

💊 Secret Medicine

Current Scene: Ready for Kanalet Castle.
📍 3.2 — Kanalet Castle

🍃 Golden Leaves

  • Leaf #1: Crow + rock.
  • Leaf #2: Mad Bomber.
  • Leaf #3: Darknuts.
  • Leaf #4: Bomb wall symbols.
  • Leaf #5: Ball & Chain Trooper.
Current Scene: All 5 leaves collected.
📍 3.3 — Seashell Collection
  • Collect Seashells #6–#13.
  • Use Shovel near Owl Statues.
  • Navigate Pothole Field exactly.
  • Obtain [Slime Key].
Current Scene: 13 Seashells. Enter Key Cavern.
📍 3.4 — Key Cavern

🗝️ Setup

  • Get Stone Beak + Map.
  • Farm keys in 4 directions.

👾 Mini-Boss: Dodongo

  • Feed bombs → 3 each.

👟 Pegasus Boots

  • Solve block puzzle → get boots.
  • Use dash to break blocks.

🗝️ Nightmare Key

  • Bomb arrow wall → drop.
  • Dash + Jump combo → reach chest.

💀 Boss: Slime Eyes

  • Dash wall → drop boss.
  • Split with dash.
  • Jump + attack → defeat.
Current Scene: Key Cavern complete. Proceed to Chapter 4.
